About Me

My work spans diverse domains, from hands-on design and collaborative research to mentoring and teaching.

This portfolio is organized thematically to reflect the way I approach design: as a flexible and iterative process shaped by context, constraints, and understanding.

Rather than showing isolated case studies, I’ve chosen to highlight recurring patterns in my work across a range of projects.

Threads of Work

Explore the core themes that shape my approach to design. Each thread highlights key methods and decision points that span across projects.

These patterns reflect how I think, collaborate, and build meaningful experiences.

Discovery

Every project starts with an understanding of context — people, environments, limitations, and goals.

I begin by exploring what already exists, what’s missing, and what matters most to the people affected. This includes:

  • Reviewing existing systems, services, and workflows
  • Identifying stakeholder goals and constraints
  • Understanding technical, organizational, and cultural constraints
  • Noting emotional or cognitive friction experienced by users
